How it's made

The usual route
Step 1

The song

One human writes the idea. Claude drafts lyrics and a style prompt. Suno generates takes until one is right.

Step 2

The faces

Each act has a locked character sheet. ChatGPT renders scene stills from it, one prompt at a time.

Step 3

The timing

The vocal stem gives word-by-word timing. The drum stem gives the beat grid. Every cut lands on one or the other.

Step 4

The edit

Claude cuts the video in code: camera moves, parallax, lyrics, overlays. Stills over full motion is a style choice.

Step 5

The story

Each release gets a write-up on Substack about what it is and how it came together.
